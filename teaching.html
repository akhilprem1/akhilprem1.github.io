Teaching Philosophy

I have served as a teaching assistant for a wide range of undergraduate and graduate physics courses, from classical mechanics to quantum field theory. I enjoy helping students build intuition by connecting abstract formalism to concrete examples and visualizations, and I like drawing parallels between ideas in physics and modern machine learning.
